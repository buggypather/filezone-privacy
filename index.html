File Zone privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

File Zone is a file manager, home screen and set of tools for Android, made by buggypather (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use File Zone, and the rights you have under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California law (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA).

The short version

  • Your files, notes, photos, recordings, messages and notifications stay on your phone. File Zone has no account and no server of its own, and we don’t receive them.
  • Ads are never personalised. File Zone doesn’t read your phone’s advertising ID, and doesn’t build a profile of you for advertisers.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information, as California law defines those words.
  • Information leaves your phone only when a feature you use needs it (listed below) or when you choose to send something.

Who is responsible

The controller for File Zone is buggypather. Contact: vance1412@proton.me. We answer privacy requests within one month (45 days for California requests).

What stays on your phone

File Zone works on these only on your phone. We don’t collect them, and they aren’t sent anywhere unless you choose to send them:

  • Files and folders you browse, open, copy, move, edit or delete.
  • Notes, voice recordings, screen and camera recordings, edited pictures.
  • Which apps you use and for how long (with Usage access), your notifications (with Notification access), and which app is in front (with the Accessibility service, which never reads the screen or what you type).
  • Text read from pictures, and translations: done on the phone by Google’s ML Kit.
  • Problem reports: saved in Documents/File Zone/Crash reports. Nothing is sent unless you send a report yourself, for example by email.
  • Sign-ins to cloud accounts you add (Google Drive, Dropbox, MEGA, WebDAV, FTP, SFTP): encrypted by your phone (Android Keystore) and used only to talk to that service.

What leaves your phone, and why

File Zone doesn’t send your files, contacts, messages, photos, recordings or precise location anywhere except as above.

Ad consent in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, Google’s consent form appears before any ad is requested. If you don’t agree, Google shows only limited ads that don’t use storage on your phone, or none. You can change your answer at any time in File Zone → Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices.

Future optional statistics

We may later add optional usage statistics to help improve File Zone. They will be off until you turn them on, and this policy will be updated before they arrive.

Permissions

File Zone asks for powerful permissions only for the features that need them, and explains each one first: All files access (file manager), Accessibility (corner buttons and taskbar), Usage access (Open apps screen), Notification access (notification panel). You can turn any of them off in Android’s settings, and the rest of File Zone keeps working.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Showing ads: our legitimate interest in funding a free app, using only non-personalised ads; and your consent, where the law requires it for storage on your phone.
  • Weather, radio, cloud accounts: providing the feature you asked for.
  • Problem reports and emails you send us: our legitimate interest in fixing problems and answering you.

How long information is kept

We don’t keep anything from your phone, because it isn’t sent to us. Emails and problem reports you send are kept until the matter is resolved, and at most 12 months. Google and the other services keep what they receive under their own policies.

International transfers

Google may process ad information outside your country, including in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its use (including the legitimate interest above), and to receive it in a portable form. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier use. You can complain to your local data protection authority. Since we don’t hold information from your phone, most requests are answered by the controls on your phone: uninstalling File Zone deletes everything it stored.

California

In the last 12 months, File Zone’s ad service received these categories of personal information: identifiers (IP address, app-set ID), internet or network activity (ad views and taps), and general location derived from the IP address. They were used to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, and were not sold or shared for cross-context behavioural advertising. We don’t use or disclose sensitive personal information to infer anything about you.

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete or correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing (there is none), to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. File Zone honours Global Privacy Control signals, which in its case changes nothing, because nothing is sold or shared. To make a request, email vance1412@proton.me; an authorised agent can make one for you with your written permission. We may ask you to confirm the request to protect you.

Children

File Zone isn’t designed for children under 13, or under 16 in the EU and UK, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Everything File Zone sends uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), except plain FTP and HTTP addresses you enter yourself. Sign-ins are encrypted on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Important changes will also be announced in the app.
